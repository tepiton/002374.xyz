# [{{ title }}](/)

A reading edition of *The Great Gatsby*, installed from [Project Gutenberg eBook #64317](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/64317). The text of the nine chapters is presented as it stands in that source; each chapter is titled with its own opening words. See the [colophon](/colophon/) for edition and license details.

The book's front matter — the dedication and the epigraph — stands on the [homepage](/).

## Typography

Set in [p22-stickley-pro-text](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/p22-stickley-pro) and [neue-kabel](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/neue-kabel) via Adobe Fonts. Body text at a fluid size scaling between 16px and 20px. Line measure constrained to 52 characters.
